Born in İskenderun on September 5, 2000. I entered Iskenderun Technical University in 2019 as the top-ranked student and graduated with a degree in Computer Engineering in 2024.

My iOS career started at the Turkcell "Geleceği Yazanlar" bootcamp, followed by an internship at Atmoswere (Turkcell Group) where I worked on the Fizy team alongside experienced engineers.

In September 2025, I opened my own office and started building and shipping apps fully independently. 19 apps later, I'm still at it.
